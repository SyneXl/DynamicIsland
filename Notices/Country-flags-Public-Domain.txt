Upstream states that these flag images are in the public domain.
Original source artwork is from Wikimedia Commons and contributors.

Bundled PNGs were rendered locally from that revision's SVGs at 100px width
with resvg_py 0.5.0. No image download or country geolocation is performed
at runtime. Per-file source and output SHA-256 values: Assets/Flags/SOURCE.json.
